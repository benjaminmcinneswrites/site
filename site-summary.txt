Better Site - Site Summary

URL: https://bettersite.co.nz
Contact: contact@bettersite.co.nz
Last updated: 2026-08-01

Better Site by Benjamin is a New Zealand website service for small businesses, local brands, consultants, and service businesses. It offers affordable business websites, website redesigns/glow-ups, managed hosting, website care plans, and SEO content updates.

The first step is simple: request a free homepage mockup. Benjamin reviews the business and creates a working homepage preview within 3 business days. The mockup is a temporary live preview link, not a complete free website, file handover, source-file export, editable design package, or transfer of ownership. There is no obligation to continue, and the preview link may be removed or expire if the customer does not continue.

The main website offers are:

- Better Business Site: from $149 NZD, including up to 7 pages, with additional pages from $14 each
- Redesign / Glow-Up: from $99 NZD

The written quote confirms the exact scope, total, and GST treatment before paid work starts.

Three hosting and care options are listed below the two website offers on the Pricing page: Starter Care at $14.99 monthly, Standard Care at $24.99 monthly, and Priority Care at $49.99 monthly. Every care plan includes one available .co.nz domain, backups, minor support, recurring content changes, and unlimited price changes.

Better Site works with businesses across New Zealand and is available for calls nationwide.

The site shows one real client project, Bottle Lake Bikes, and several demo websites. Demo sites should be treated as examples, not real client businesses.
