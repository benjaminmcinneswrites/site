Better Site - Site Summary

URL: https://bettersite.co.nz
Contact: contact@bettersite.co.nz
Last updated: 2026-06-05

Better Site by Benjamin is a New Zealand website service for small businesses, local brands, consultants, and service businesses. It offers affordable one-page websites, fuller business websites, website redesigns/glow-ups, managed hosting, separate website care plans, and SEO content updates.

The site is built around a simple first step: request a free homepage mockup. Benjamin reviews the business and creates a homepage direction within 3 business days. The mockup is a preview, not a complete free website, and there is no obligation to continue.

The main website offers are:

- Starter One-Page Site: $199
- Better Business Site: $499
- Redesign / Glow-Up: $249

Care plans still exist on a separate page, but they are not one of the three main website offers on the pricing page.

Better Site works with businesses across New Zealand and is available for calls nationwide.

The site shows one real client project, Bottle Lake Bikes, and several demo website directions. Demo sites should be treated as examples, not real client businesses.
