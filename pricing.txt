## Payment Notes

The free homepage mockup is free and is delivered within 3 business days according to the website. It is a temporary live homepage preview link, not a complete free website, file handover, source-file export, editable design package, or transfer of ownership. If the customer does not continue with paid work, the preview link may be removed or expire.

If a customer wants to continue after the mockup, the accepted quote should confirm the scope, any upfront payment, and the remaining balance due before delivery or launch.

## Separate Care Plans

Care plans still exist on a separate page, but they are not one of the three main website offers on the pricing page. If a business needs hosting, care, content edits, backups, or SEO content updates after launch, Better Site can quote or discuss that separately.

## Notes

Extra pages, ecommerce, booking embeds, integrations, custom functionality, advanced forms, hosting, domains, email hosting, ongoing care, and larger redesign work can be quoted separately.

Owner review is recommended for GST/tax wording, exact payment terms, support boundaries, hosting provider details, domain process, and whether any quoted price includes or excludes third-party costs.
