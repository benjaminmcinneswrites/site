## Payment Notes

The free homepage mockup is free and is delivered within 3 business days according to the website. It is a temporary live homepage preview link, not a complete free website, file handover, source-file export, editable design package, or transfer of ownership. If the customer does not continue with paid work, the preview link may be removed or expire.

If a customer wants to continue after the mockup, the accepted quote should confirm the scope, any upfront payment, and the remaining balance due before delivery or launch.
