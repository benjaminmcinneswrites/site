## Payment Notes

It is a temporary live homepage preview link, not a complete free website, file handover, source-file export, editable design package, or transfer of ownership. If the customer does not continue with paid work, the preview link may be removed or expire.

If a customer wants to continue after the mockup, the accepted quote should confirm the scope, any upfront payment, and the remaining balance due before delivery or launch.

## Hosting and Care Plans

Every monthly plan includes managed hosting, one available .co.nz domain (subject to availability), backups, minor support, recurring content changes, and unlimited price changes.

- Starter Care: $14.99 NZD per month. Up to 3 edits, revisions, or content changes per month.
- Standard Care: $24.99 NZD per month. Up to 5 edits per month and priority support.
- Priority Care: $49.99 NZD per month. Up to 10 substantial edits per month, highest-priority support, same-day updates within 24 hours, monthly SEO content optimisation, and an admin dashboard for quick update submissions.

An included edit can be a substantial content replacement, image change, rewritten section, notice, or similar content update. Price changes are unlimited and do not use the monthly edit allowance.
