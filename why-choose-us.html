Why choose Better Site

More thoughtful than a website builder. Simpler than a big agency.

Using a website builder can feel like the quickest way to get online, while a big agency can feel like the safest way to get something polished.

For many small businesses, the better option sits in the middle: brand-focused strategy, thoughtful design, and direct communication without the agency price tag or endless back-and-forth nightmares.

What matters most

Seven reasons businesses choose Better Site.

A Clearer First Impression

Your website should instantly show people that your business is real and worth contacting. I focus on making sites that feel branded rather than generic, with carefully planned copywriting and visual decisions that match the business you are building.

Clearer Paths to Enquiries

Visitors should quickly understand what you do, why it matters, and how to take the next step. I think carefully about structure, headings, buttons, and page flow so the site feels natural to navigate, especially for people viewing it on their phone.

Better Foundations for Being Found

A well SEO-optimised website needs clear page titles, useful headings, relevant keywords, fast loading, and a sensible structure that search engines can understand. I build with these basics in mind so your site has a stronger starting point.

Pricing, Clear Scope

Better Site keeps the main offers focused: a Starter One-Page Site, a fuller Better Business Site, and a Redesign / Glow-Up for existing websites. Hosting, domains, care, extra pages, and custom features can be discussed separately when you need them.

A Simpler Way to Get a Professional Website

You don't need to deal with the overpriced, back-and-forth communication nightmare of big agencies. My service is practical for New Zealand small businesses who prioritize clear design, thoughtful wording, and ease of use.

Made Around Your Business, Not Squeezed Into a Template

DIY builders can be useful, but they often lead to websites that feel as if they could belong to anyone. Every business is different, and every business has a brand: specific services or products, customer base, and reasons people choose you. I bring design and writing together to build you a site that feels specific, human-authored, and aligned with what it is you actually offer.

Simple, but still specific.

The goal is not to fill a template. It is to make the business feel clearer, easier to trust, and easier to contact.
