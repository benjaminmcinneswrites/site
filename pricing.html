Hosting and care termsAsk about care

The details that affect the final quote.

Are hosting and a domain included?

Not automatically. Hosting can be selected separately, and domain costs or setup are confirmed where needed.

How many pages are in the business package?

It is a multi-page starting package. The sitemap and final page count are confirmed in the written quote.

Can I add ecommerce or custom features?

Yes, where suitable. Ecommerce, advanced integrations and custom functionality are quoted separately.

Can you care for a website you did not build?

Often, yes. I review the current setup first to confirm what can be supported and what the plan should cover.

Can I change or cancel a monthly plan?

The applicable notice, billing and cancellation terms are set out in the Hosting & Care Plan Terms and confirmed before the plan starts.

What do I need to provide?

Accurate business information, useful source content, images where available, and timely feedback or approvals.
