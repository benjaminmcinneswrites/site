Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026Cookies and browser storage

How this site uses cookies

Better Site uses cookies, browser storage, and similar technologies only where they support normal website operation, security, form delivery, performance, and a smoother browsing experience.

These technologies can be set by the website itself or by service providers that help host the site, protect forms, deliver messages, or keep basic technical records.

Browser storage used by the site

The site's first-party JavaScript uses session storage to make internal page transitions feel smoother. This stores a simple temporary value in your browser during the browsing session.

Session storage is not the same as a tracking cookie. This storage is not used to identify you, build a profile about you, or track you across other websites.

Contact forms and third-party processing

The contact and free homepage mockup request forms submit through FormSubmit. When you submit a form, FormSubmit and related email systems may process the information you enter, along with normal technical information needed to deliver the message.

Third-party services may use their own cookies, logs, spam checks, or security technologies when processing submissions or protecting their services.

Server logs

Like most websites, the hosting provider may keep server logs. These can include technical information such as IP address, page requests, browser type, device information, timestamps, and error details. These logs are generally used for security, troubleshooting, performance, and basic operation.

How you can control cookies

You can usually block, delete, or limit cookies and browser storage in your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may affect website forms, security checks, or normal site behaviour.

You can also avoid submitting forms and email Better Site directly at contact@bettersite.co.nz.
