Is the homepage mockup really free?

Yes. It is a temporary live homepage preview, with no payment and no obligation to continue.

Is it a complete free website?

No. It is a homepage preview, not a finished website, file handover or transfer of ownership.

What is the difference between the website options?

The Better Business Site starts from $349 and includes up to 7 pages, with additional pages from $14 each, plus 3 months of free hosting. The $149 Redesign / Glow-Up includes 3 months of free hosting if needed.

Is copywriting included?

Help organising and improving your supplied text is included. Full copywriting is quoted separately.

Are hosting and a domain included?

Both website options include 3 months of free hosting; the redesign includes it if needed. After that, every monthly care plan includes managed hosting, one available .co.nz domain, scheduled backups and support while the plan remains active.

Are the prices in NZD, and what about GST?

Prices are shown in NZD. The written quote confirms the final total and GST treatment before you accept it.

Is SEO included?

SEO setup such as titles, descriptions, headings and logically arranged pages is included where applicable. Rankings, traffic and enquiries are not guaranteed.

Can I add extra pages or features?

Yes. Extra pages, ecommerce, booking embeds, integrations, advanced forms and custom functionality can be quoted separately.

How long does it take?

The homepage mockup is prepared within 3 business days. A full website timeline depends on scope, content, feedback and approvals.

Do you work outside Christchurch?

Yes. Better Site works with businesses across New Zealand and calls are available nationwide.

Do I have to continue after the mockup?

No. If you like the preview, you can review a written quote. If not, you can leave it there.

Who owns the finished paid website?

Once the agreed website fee is paid in full, ownership follows the accepted quote and Terms of Service. The free preview itself is not transferred.
