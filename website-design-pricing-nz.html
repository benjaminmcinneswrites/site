What affects the quote

The right website price depends on what the site has to do.

Page count matters, but so does the amount of writing, service detail, SEO content, forms, integrations, existing-site cleanup, and post-launch support your business needs.

Fit

Scope and Sections

A Starter site stays focused on one page and up to 5 sections. A fuller business site can carry more structure and service detail.

Copy

Copy and Content

These package prices work best when you provide the text, images, and business facts. Advanced copywriting can be quoted separately.

Add

Extras and Ongoing Costs

Hosting, domains, ecommerce, booking tools, email hosting, extra pages, and ongoing care are separate unless your quote says otherwise.
