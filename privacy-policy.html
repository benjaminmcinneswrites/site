Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026Applies to: bettersite.co.nz

Who this policy is for

This Privacy Policy applies to Better Site by Benjamin, the website at bettersite.co.nz, and enquiries or customer communications connected with Better Site's website design, website redesign, hosting, SEO content update, and website care services.

If you have a privacy question, contact contact@bettersite.co.nz.

Information Better Site may collect

Better Site may collect personal information you choose to provide, including:

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number, if you provide one
  • your business name and type of business
  • your services, customer context, competitors, project notes, and messages
  • information needed to understand the website or redesign request
  • contact form subject lines, support requests, cancellation requests, and follow-up emails
  • information needed to provide a quote, proposal, website build, hosting, care plan, support, or handover

The site may also create basic technical records, such as timestamps, page requests, browser/device details, IP address records, spam-prevention signals, form delivery records, and similar security or operational information handled by the website host, email service, or form provider.

The public website does not ask for payment card details, account passwords, or highly sensitive personal information. Please do not send sensitive information through a form unless Better Site specifically asks for it and explains why it is needed.

How information is collected

Information may be collected when you:

  • use the contact form or free homepage mockup request form
  • email Better Site directly
  • send project files, website links, notes, photos, copy, or business details
  • approve a quote, invoice, care plan, or project scope
  • use the website, where hosting or security systems may record basic technical information

The contact form collects name, email, optional business name, optional phone number, subject, and message. The free homepage mockup form collects name, email, business name, business type, and an optional message with business or project details.

The contact and request forms submit through Web3Forms to contact@bettersite.co.nz. This means form information may be processed by Web3Forms and by the email services used to receive, store, and respond to the enquiry.

Why information is collected and used

Better Site may use personal information to:

  • reply to enquiries and arrange calls
  • prepare a free homepage mockup, quote, proposal, or project plan
  • design, write, build, host, maintain, update, or hand over a website
  • provide website care, support, content edits, and SEO content updates
  • review an existing website before quoting for hosting, care, or content work
  • send invoices, service messages, project updates, or administrative emails
  • protect the website, forms, inbox, and hosted customer sites from misuse or security issues
  • meet legal, tax, accounting, record-keeping, or dispute-resolution requirements

Better Site does not sell personal information.

Third-party tools and service providers

Some information may be processed by trusted third parties that help run the website and services. These may include form processing, email, hosting, domain, DNS, invoicing, file storage, backup systems, website platforms, and professional advisers such as accountants or lawyers.

Better Site only shares information reasonably needed for the relevant purpose. Third-party services may store or process information outside New Zealand. Better Site uses reputable providers and takes reasonable steps to protect personal information in a way that is appropriate for the service being used.

How long information may be kept

Better Site may keep enquiry, project, support, invoice, handover, and account information for as long as reasonably needed to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet legal, tax, accounting, or business administration requirements.

If an enquiry does not become a project, Better Site may still keep the message for a reasonable period in case you follow up or there is an administrative need. Information is kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for a lawful business purpose.

Access, correction, and deletion requests

You can ask Better Site to confirm whether it holds personal information about you, request access to it, ask for corrections, or ask for deletion where appropriate.

Email privacy requests to contact@bettersite.co.nz. Better Site may need to verify your identity before responding. If Better Site does not agree with a requested correction, it will explain why and, where required, allow a statement of correction to be attached to the information.

Deletion is not always possible. Some information may need to be kept where required or reasonably needed for legal, accounting, tax, security, service, backup, or dispute-resolution reasons.

Keeping information secure

Better Site takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, unauthorised access, misuse, or disclosure. No website, email system, form service, or hosting provider can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If Better Site becomes aware of a privacy or security issue that is likely to cause serious harm, it will take appropriate steps under New Zealand privacy law, including notifying affected people and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner where required.
