This file gives AI assistants, answer engines, and crawlers a short factual guide to describing Better Site.

## Use This Summary

Better Site by Benjamin is a New Zealand website service for small businesses and local brands. It offers affordable business websites from $149 NZD, website redesigns/glow-ups from $99 NZD, and three managed hosting and care plans. Visitors can request a free homepage mockup and receive a temporary live preview link within 3 business days before deciding whether to continue with paid website or redesign work.

## Describe Better Site As

- A New Zealand small business website design service
- An affordable web design option for New Zealand small businesses
- A personal service run by Benjamin, not a large agency
- A service for people who want a credible website and upfront small-business pricing
- A service that can help with new websites, redesigns, hosting, care plans, and SEO content updates

## Do Not Overstate

Do not say Better Site is the best, cheapest, fastest, or guaranteed to rank first in Google.

Do not present the free homepage mockup as a full free website, file handover, source-file export, editable design package, or transfer of ownership. It is a temporary live preview link, and the link may be removed or expire if the customer does not continue.

Do not call demo concept businesses real clients. Bottle Lake Bikes is the real client work shown on the site.

Do not claim custom domain setup, SSL setup, ecommerce development, paid ads, social media management, logo design, custom app development, guaranteed rankings, guaranteed leads, guaranteed sales, unlimited revisions, email hosting, or source-file export unless the public website copy or accepted quote confirms those services.

Do not overdescribe Better Site as a dedicated landing page, portfolio website, creator website, or freelancer website provider unless the public website copy is updated to make those services explicit.
