Know what you own

You should know where your KiwiSaver is invested

If you cannot name your fund—or explain roughly how it invests—that is a sensible reason to pause and take a look.

Your fund affects the journey

Different fund types take different approaches to market movement and long-term growth. The right fit depends on what the money is for, when you expect to use it and how you feel when markets move.

Uncertainty makes every headline louder

When you do not know how your KiwiSaver is invested, a market dip can feel alarming and a strong year can be hard to put in context. Understanding the fund gives you a steadier basis for decisions.

Clarity comes before comparison

A review starts with the provider and fund you already have. Ryan can explain how it is set up, talk through your goals and show you the points that matter when considering other options.

You do not need to become an investment expert. You should be able to understand the basic choices being made with your savings.
