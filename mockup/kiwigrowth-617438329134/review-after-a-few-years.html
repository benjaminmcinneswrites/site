A useful time to check in

It has been a few years since you checked your fund

That does not automatically mean something is wrong. It does mean the choices made then may no longer reflect the life you have now.

Life can move faster than your fund settings

A new job, different income, a first-home plan or a shorter path to retirement can all change what you need from KiwiSaver. Your account can keep running in the background while those important details move on.

A review brings the setup back into view

A useful check looks at your current provider and fund type, the timeframe around your goals and how comfortable you are with investment ups and downs. It gives you a clear picture of what you have before you decide whether anything needs attention.

Checking does not mean changing

Sometimes a review confirms that the current setup still makes sense. If there are options worth considering, Ryan can explain them in plain language so the next decision is yours.

If KiwiSaver has been out of sight for a while, a short conversation can make it understandable again.
