Quick answers to commonly asked questions.

KiwiGrowth reviews your needs and current KiwiSaver setup, considers options within its disclosed advice scope, and explains suitable next steps clearly. If a transfer is relevant and you choose to proceed, KiwiGrowth can help with the process.

The review is free and no-obligation, with no additional client fee for the review. KiwiGrowth is compensated by providers it recommends. Read the Public Disclosure for complete remuneration and conflict information.

KiwiGrowth receives commissions from KiwiSaver providers on whose products it gives financial advice. The Public Disclosure explains the remuneration and how conflicts of interest are managed.

Ryan will explain the available options so you can make an informed decision. Changing provider or fund is not automatic or required.

KiwiGrowth’s current guidance says a provider transfer typically takes 7–10 business days. Actual timing can vary, so this is not guaranteed.

KiwiGrowth’s current guidance says you do not need to notify your employer after changing provider. Employee and employer contributions are paid to Inland Revenue, which directs them to your designated KiwiSaver provider.
