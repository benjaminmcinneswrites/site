A new chapter

When life changes, your KiwiSaver plan deserves a fresh look

Your original setup was based on an earlier version of your income, goals and timeframe. A meaningful change can make a review worthwhile.

A new job can change the numbers

A move in salary, employment or contribution rate can alter how much is flowing into KiwiSaver. It is a natural point to check what is happening and whether the wider setup still suits you.

A new goal can change the timeframe

First-home plans, retirement timing and other priorities can bring the intended use of the money closer. Timeframe is one of the most important parts of any conversation about fund choice.

You do not need a crisis to review

A review is simply a chance to reconnect the account with the life it is meant to support. If the current setup still fits, you leave with reassurance. If it does not, you leave knowing what to consider next.

KiwiSaver can keep moving quietly while life changes around it. A review puts the two back in the same conversation.
