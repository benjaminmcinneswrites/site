# Better Site Services

Website: https://bettersite.co.nz
Contact: contact@bettersite.co.nz
Last updated: 2026-08-01

Better Site provides affordable business websites, website redesigns/glow-ups, managed hosting, website care, and SEO content updates for New Zealand small businesses and local brands.

## Main Website Offers

### Better Business Site

The Better Business Site is the main website package. It is for businesses that want clear service detail, strong contact options, and a more established website. The starting price is from $149 NZD and includes up to 7 pages. Additional pages start from $14 NZD each.

Typical inclusions include mobile-first design, services and offer sections, a contact form and enquiry CTA, SEO setup, and up to three revision rounds.

### Redesign / Glow-Up

Better Site helps businesses with outdated, confusing, or hard-to-trust websites. The Redesign / Glow-Up offer starts from $99 NZD and can include an updated homepage design, improved layout, easier scanning, mobile-friendly improvements where applicable, better CTA placement, SEO/meta cleanup where applicable, and up to three revision rounds.

Useful page:

- https://bettersite.co.nz/website-redesign-nz

## Exclusions and Limits

The current main offers do not include ecommerce, custom backend/app functionality, email hosting, unlimited revisions, complex animations, guaranteed SEO rankings, guaranteed traffic, guaranteed leads, or guaranteed sales.

Extra pages, booking embeds, integrations, ecommerce, custom functionality, advanced copywriting, hosting, domains, email hosting, and ongoing care can be quoted separately where appropriate.

## Managed Website Hosting

Better Site offers hosting for new or existing websites as a separate service or through care plans. Hosting should not be described as included in the main website offers unless the accepted quote says so.

## Website Care Plans

Care plans sit alongside website project pricing and are for businesses that want the technical side handled after launch or for an existing website. Every plan includes hosting, one available .co.nz domain, backups, minor support, recurring content changes, and unlimited price changes. Monthly allowances are 3 changes on Starter Care ($14.99), 5 on Standard Care ($24.99), and 10 substantial changes on Priority Care ($49.99), with progressively faster support and additional Priority Care tools.
