# Better Site Services

Website: https://bettersite.co.nz
Contact: contact@bettersite.co.nz
Last updated: 2026-06-05

Better Site provides affordable website design, one-page websites, fuller small business websites, website redesigns/glow-ups, managed hosting, website care, and SEO content updates for New Zealand small businesses and local brands.

## Main Website Offers

### Starter One-Page Site

Better Site offers clean one-page websites for sole traders, creators, local services, and new businesses that need a proper online presence without a big budget. The visible price is $199.

Typical inclusions include one page, up to 5 focused sections, mobile-friendly design, a basic contact section, a basic SEO title and description, one revision round, and simple launch support. The client provides text and images.

### Better Business Site

The Better Business Site is the main popular package. It is for businesses that want more structure, clearer messaging, services & offer sections, enquiry paths, and a more polished online presence. The visible price is $499.

Typical inclusions include a multi-page site, mobile-friendly design, services & offer sections, contact form & enquiry CTA, basic SEO setup, and two revision rounds.

### Redesign / Glow-Up

Better Site helps businesses with outdated, unclear, or hard-to-trust websites. The Redesign / Glow-Up offer is $249 and can focus on cleaner design direction, improved layout, better visual hierarchy, mobile-friendly improvements where applicable, better CTA placement, and basic SEO/meta cleanup where applicable.

Useful page:

- https://bettersite.co.nz/website-redesign-nz

## Exclusions and Limits

The current main offers do not include ecommerce, custom backend/app functionality, email hosting, unlimited revisions, complex animations, guaranteed SEO rankings, guaranteed traffic, guaranteed leads, or guaranteed sales.

Extra pages, booking embeds, integrations, ecommerce, custom functionality, advanced copywriting, hosting, domains, email hosting, and ongoing care can be quoted separately where appropriate.
