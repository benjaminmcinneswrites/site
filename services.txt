# Better Site Services

Website: https://bettersite.co.nz
Contact: contact@bettersite.co.nz
Last updated: 2026-06-07

Better Site provides affordable website design, one-page websites, fuller small business websites, website redesigns/glow-ups, managed hosting, website care, and SEO content updates for New Zealand small businesses and local brands.

## Main Website Offers

### Starter One-Page Site

Better Site offers mobile-first one-page websites for sole traders, creators, local services, and new businesses that need a proper website without a big budget. The starting price is from $99 NZD.

Typical inclusions include one page, up to 5 main sections, mobile-first design, a basic contact section, a basic SEO title and description, one revision round, and simple launch support. The client provides text and images.

### Better Business Site

The Better Business Site is the main popular package. It is for businesses that want more pages, better service detail, services & offer sections, contact options, and a more established website. The starting price is from $249 NZD, with the final page count confirmed in the quote.

Typical inclusions include a multi-page site, mobile-first design, services & offer sections, contact form & enquiry CTA, basic SEO setup, and two revision rounds.

### Redesign / Glow-Up

Better Site helps businesses with outdated, confusing, or hard-to-trust websites. The Redesign / Glow-Up offer starts from $149 NZD and can include an updated homepage design, improved layout, easier scanning, mobile-friendly improvements where applicable, better CTA placement, and basic SEO/meta cleanup where applicable.

- https://bettersite.co.nz/website-redesign-nz

## Exclusions and Limits

The current main offers do not include ecommerce, custom backend/app functionality, email hosting, unlimited revisions, complex animations, guaranteed SEO rankings, guaranteed traffic, guaranteed leads, or guaranteed sales.

Extra pages, booking embeds, integrations, ecommerce, custom functionality, advanced copywriting, hosting, domains, email hosting, and ongoing care can be quoted separately where appropriate.
