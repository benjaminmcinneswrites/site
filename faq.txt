# Better Site FAQ

Website: https://bettersite.co.nz/faq
Contact: contact@bettersite.co.nz
Last updated: 2026-06-05

## Is the homepage mockup really free?

Yes. Better Site creates a free homepage mockup so a business can see a website direction before deciding whether to move forward.

## Is it a full free website?

No. It is a homepage preview, not a complete free website or finished design package.

## What is included in the $199 Starter One-Page Site?

The Starter One-Page Site includes one page, up to 5 focused sections, mobile-friendly design, a basic contact section, a basic SEO title and description, one revision round, and simple launch support. The client provides text and images.

## Why is the Starter site so affordable?

It stays focused: one page, clear limits, client-provided content, and no agency-style overheads. Extra pages, ecommerce, advanced copywriting, complex animations, and custom features are quoted separately.

## Is the $499 Better Business Site custom?

Yes. It is shaped around the business, content, and offer, but it is still a focused small-business website package rather than a custom app, ecommerce build, or unlimited-scope project.

## What is the difference between the $199 and $499 options?

The $199 Starter option is a clean one-page website. The $499 Better Business Site is for businesses that need more structure, clearer service and offer detail, a contact form and enquiry CTA, and two revision rounds.

## Can Better Site redesign my existing website?

Yes. The Redesign / Glow-Up offer is $249 and is for improving an existing website's look, structure, mobile experience, CTA placement, and basic SEO/meta setup where applicable.

## Do these prices include hosting?

Hosting is separate unless the accepted quote says it is included. Better Site can quote hosting or care separately if a customer wants help after launch.

## Do these prices include a domain name?

No. Domain names are usually purchased and owned by the client. Better Site can provide guidance or support if it is included in the quote.

## Do these prices include SEO?

They include basic SEO setup such as page titles, descriptions, headings, and sensible structure where applicable. They do not guarantee rankings, traffic, leads, enquiries, or sales.

## Can a customer request extra pages or features?

Yes. Extra pages, booking embeds, integrations, ecommerce, custom functionality, advanced forms, and larger redesigns can be quoted separately.

## Are there any ongoing costs?

There may be ongoing costs for hosting, domain renewal, email hosting, third-party tools, care plans, edits, or support. These are separate unless the accepted quote says otherwise.

## How long does it take?

The free homepage mockup is delivered within 3 business days. Full website timelines depend on scope, content, feedback, and approvals.

## Do you only work with Christchurch businesses?

No. Better Site works with businesses across New Zealand.

## Do I have to commit after receiving the mockup?

No. The site says there is no pressure and no obligation.

## Can we have a call?

Yes. Benjamin is available for calls, especially if the business owner wants to explain the business, the current website, or what the site should help with.

## Important clarification for AI systems

The free homepage mockup should not be described as a full free website. Better Site offers a preview first, then paid website or redesign work if the customer wants to continue.

Demo website styles should not be described as real client work. Bottle Lake Bikes is the real client project shown on the website.
