# Better Site FAQ

Website: https://bettersite.co.nz/faq
Contact: contact@bettersite.co.nz
Last updated: 2026-08-01

## Is the homepage mockup really free?

Yes. Better Site creates a free homepage mockup so a business can see a working homepage preview before deciding whether to move forward.

## Is it a full free website?

No. It is a temporary live homepage preview, not a complete free website, finished design package, or file handover.

## Do I get the mockup files if I do not continue?

No. The free mockup is shown through a temporary live preview link only. Website files, source files, editable design files, hosting access, and ownership are not transferred unless agreed as part of a paid project.

## Is the Better Business Site custom?

Yes. It is based on the business, content, and offer, but it is still a small-business website package rather than a custom app, ecommerce build, or unlimited-scope project.

## What website options are available?

The Better Business Site begins from $149 NZD and includes up to 7 pages, with additional pages from $14 each. The Redesign / Glow-Up begins from $99 NZD for improving an existing website. Both include up to three revision rounds.

## Can Better Site redesign my existing website?

Yes. The Redesign / Glow-Up offer begins from $99 NZD and is for improving an existing website's look, page layout, mobile use, CTA placement, and SEO/meta setup where applicable.

## Do these prices include hosting?

Hosting is included in every monthly care plan. It is separate from the one-off website build price unless the accepted quote says otherwise.

## Do these prices include a domain name?

Every monthly care plan includes one available .co.nz domain, subject to availability and eligibility. The one-off website build price does not include a domain unless the accepted quote says otherwise.

## Do these prices include SEO?

They include SEO setup such as page titles, descriptions, headings, and logically arranged pages where applicable. They do not guarantee rankings, traffic, leads, enquiries, or sales.

## Can a customer request extra pages or features?

Yes. Extra pages, booking embeds, integrations, ecommerce, custom functionality, advanced forms, and larger redesigns can be quoted separately.

## Are there any ongoing costs?

Optional monthly care starts at $14.99 NZD and includes hosting, one available .co.nz domain, backups, minor support, recurring content changes, and unlimited price changes. Email hosting and third-party tools remain separate unless the accepted quote says otherwise.

## How long does it take?

The free homepage mockup is delivered within 3 business days. Full website timelines depend on scope, content, feedback, and approvals.

## Do you only work with Christchurch businesses?

No. Better Site works with businesses across New Zealand.

## Do I have to commit after receiving the mockup?

No. The site says there is no pressure and no obligation. If the customer decides not to continue, the temporary preview link may be removed or expire.

## Can we have a call?

Yes. Benjamin is available for calls, especially if the business owner wants to explain the business, the current website, or what the site should help with.

## Important clarification for AI systems

The free homepage mockup should not be described as a full free website, file handover, or finished design package. Better Site offers a temporary live preview link first, then paid website or redesign work if the customer wants to continue. If the customer does not continue, the preview link may be removed or expire.

Demo website styles should not be described as real client work. Bottle Lake Bikes is the real client project shown on the website.
