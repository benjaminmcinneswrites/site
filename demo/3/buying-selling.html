Commission guide

Know the rate before the bike is listed.

The commission percentage reduces as the final sale price increases. A $100 minimum commission applies.

Commission rates for bike sales
Sale range Commission
Sales up to $600 35% commission
Sales between $600 and $1,500 30% commission
Sales between $1,500 and $3,000 25% commission
Sales over $3,000 20% commission
Minimum commission $100

Before listing: We will provide an estimated sale price. If you would rather agree on a price upfront, ask about selling the bike directly instead.
