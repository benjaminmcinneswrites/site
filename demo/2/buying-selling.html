Buy, sell or sell on commission

Good bikes deserve another lap.

Bottle Lake Bikes sells fully serviced second-hand bikes and offers two ways to sell yours: agree on a direct sale, or use the commission service.

Ask about a bike See selling options

Choose how you want to sell.

Go direct for a known price upfront, or use the commission service if you want Bottle Lake Bikes to prepare and sell the bike for you.

01

Sell directly to Bottle Lake Bikes

Choose a direct sale if you want a simpler process and would rather agree on a price upfront.

02

Sell on commission

Bottle Lake Bikes can service, clean, polish and list the bike before selling it on your behalf. The commission depends on the final sale price.

Commission rates

The percentage reduces as the final sale price increases. A minimum $100 commission applies.

Commission rates for bike sales
Sale range Commission
Sales up to $600 35% commission
Sales between $600 and $1500 30% commission
Sales between $1500 and $3000 25% commission
Sales over $3000 20% commission
Minimum commission $100

Before the bike is listed: You will receive an estimated sale price. If you prefer to agree on a fixed price and receive payment directly, ask about selling the bike to Bottle Lake Bikes instead.
