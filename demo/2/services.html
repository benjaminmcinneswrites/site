Regular maintenance

General Service

A mechanical check and tune to keep your bike safe, smooth and reliable.

  • Brake tuning and adjustment
  • Gear tuning for smooth shifting
  • Safety & bolt check
  • Clean bike & wheels
  • Adjust hubs & headset
  • Inflate tyres
  • Chain lubrication

Full mechanical refresh

Full Service

Price: $159

Includes everything in the General Service, plus a full service of key mechanical systems:

  • Cable lubrication or replacement (new parts extra)
  • Wheel truing & spoke tensioning
  • Hub service
  • Headset service

Add to either package

Drivetrain deep clean

Price: $39

Add a complete drivetrain strip-down and clean to either service package.

  • Full removal of chain, cassette and chainrings
  • Deep clean of all drivetrain components
  • Reassembly and lubrication
  • Replacement drivetrain parts fitted if required (parts extra)

Workshop add-ons for more specific jobs.
