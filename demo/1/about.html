Meet your mechanic

From farm-built trails to a Parklands workshop.

Hey, I’m Patrick — the guy behind Bottle Lake Bikes.

Everyday bikes, high-end builds and more

Bottle Lake Bikes repairs and services all types of bikes. I also have experience working on scooters, prams and wheelchairs, so if it rolls and needs mechanical attention, get in touch.

Whether you’re keeping a commuter running on a budget or booking a full service for a high-end mountain, gravel or road bike, you can expect personal service and clear communication.

Life gets busy, so I offer drop-off and pick-up outside standard hours by arrangement. Give me a call during the day and we can find a time that works.

A second life for good bikes

I sell fully serviced used bikes, can buy your bike outright or sell it on your behalf, and can act as a buying agent if you want help finding a good bike without ending up with a lemon.

It started with a makeshift chain whip

My journey into bikes started around age 10, building backyard trails with my brother on our parents’ farm. We kept our bikes going with the cheapest second-hand parts we could find and whatever tools were available.

I still remember tying an old chain to a workbench to make a chain whip, then swapping a snapped frame on the garage floor. Since then, I’ve worked in the cycling industry as a service technician, a design engineer and in product sourcing.

Starting Bottle Lake Bikes felt like the natural next step. My aim is simple: explain what the bike needs, keep you informed and treat the work as carefully as I would on my own bike.
