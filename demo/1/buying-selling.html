Second-hand, done properly

Buy a serviced bike. Sell yours your way.

Every used bike sold by Bottle Lake Bikes is fully serviced before sale. If you are selling, choose a direct offer for a simpler handover or have Patrick prepare and sell the bike on your behalf.

Choose your route

Three ways Bottle Lake Bikes can help.

01

Sell directly

Bottle Lake Bikes can buy your bike from you. This route suits sellers who want to agree an amount upfront and keep the handover simple.

02

Sell on commission

Patrick can service, clean, polish and list the bike for you. This route is for sellers aiming for a stronger sale result and comfortable paying commission after the sale.

03

Get help buying

If you know the kind of bike you need but want another set of eyes on the search, Patrick can act as a buying agent and help you avoid a poor purchase.

Commission sales

Know the rate before the bike is listed.

Bottle Lake Bikes will provide an estimated sale price before listing your bike. A minimum commission of $100 applies.

Commission rates by final sale price
Sale range Commission
Sales up to $600 35%
Sales between $600 and $1,500 30%
Sales between $1,500 and $3,000 25%
Sales over $3,000 20%
Minimum commission $100

Not sure which selling route fits?

Ask for a direct offer if certainty matters most. Ask about commission if you would rather have the bike prepared and sold on your behalf.
