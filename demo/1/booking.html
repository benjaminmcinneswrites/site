Before you head over

A straightforward workshop handover.

  1. 01

    Pick an appointment

    Choose the service and a suitable time in the online calendar. You can compare the listed work on the services page first.

  2. 02

    Check for confirmation

    The booking system should send a confirmation shortly after you finish. If it does not arrive, call the workshop.

  3. 03

    Bring the bike to Parklands

    The workshop is at 43 Broadhaven Ave. If you need an after-hours handover, call during the day to arrange it.
