Workshop menu

Clear pricing, from a tune-up to a full service.

Bottle Lake Bikes works on mountain, road, gravel, commuter and e-bikes. Choose a package below or book an assessment if you are not sure what the bike needs.

Package prices cover the listed workshop work. Replacement parts are extra where noted.

Core service

General service

$79

A mechanical check and tune for the parts that affect everyday safety, shifting and ride quality.

  • Brake tuning and adjustment
  • Gear tuning for smooth shifting
  • Safety & bolt check
  • Clean bike & wheels
  • Adjust hubs & headset
  • Inflate tyres
  • Chain lubrication

Deeper workshop service

Full service

$159

Everything in the general service, plus service work on the bike’s key mechanical systems.

  • Cable lubrication or replacement (new parts extra)
  • Wheel truing & spoke tensioning
  • Hub service
  • Headset service
  • Bottom bracket service

Add to either package

Drivetrain deep clean

$39

Remove built-up grime from the drivetrain before fresh lubrication and reassembly.

  • Full removal of chain, cassette and chainrings
  • Deep clean of all drivetrain components
  • Reassembly and lubrication
  • Replacement drivetrain parts fitted if required (parts extra)

Specific jobs

Add only the work your bike needs.

The following add-ons are available to suit your bike’s specific needs. We don’t include them in standard packages, so you only pay for what your bike actually requires.

Suspension, brakes and setup

  • Fork lowers - $65 per unit (plus seals if required)
  • Rear shock air can service - $65
  • Full suspension pivot clean & grease - $90 (bearing replacement available if required)
  • Dropper post service - $60 (plus parts if required)
  • Brake bleed - $25 (Shimano/Tektro), $40 (SRAM) (per brake)
  • Tyre sealant refreshed - $8 per wheel
